COMMON INQUIRIES

What should I bring on my first day at Siya Fitness Gym?

Bring clean workout shoes, a small sweat towel, a water bottle, and comfortable athletic clothing. You can store your belongings in our secure lockers.

Are beginner lifters welcome at Siya Gym?

Absolutely! Over 40% of our members started with zero gym experience. Our floor coaches will gladly walk you through machine setups and show you proper barbell form.

Do you have secure parking at Kanwe Avenue?

Yes, we have dedicated on-site parking with full-time security guards stationed during all operational hours for complete peace of mind.

Can I purchase a Single Day Pass to try out the gym?

Yes! We offer a Single Day Pass for K80. You can drop in directly at our Kanwe Avenue location or message us via WhatsApp to arrange your visit.
